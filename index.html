Assignment 2 Paragraph on Something Interesting: XCOM 2

I find the enemey design of XCOM 2 interesting in how it tricks newer players into prioritizing the wrong enemies.
an example being a group of two ADVENT Troopers and a Sectoid alien. The troopers have poor stats and can only shoot
while the Sectiod has almost three times the health, can mind control, and raise dead humanoids as zombies.
Newer playes will understandably spend their enitre turn to elminate the Sectiod, however this is a mistake
assuming that Sectoid is eliminated that turn there are still troopers that will shoot on their turn.
The trap is that the Sectoid will always favor its powers over shooting, powers that are not immediately a threat
to your soldiers as they do no damage. The Sectoids powers can also be interupted with equipment such as a flash-bang
grenade or killing it on the following turn will casue any mind control(s) to end and all rasied zombies to die. The troopers
will always shoot on their turn if they can leading to potential wounds or death of your soldiers and in XCOM when a
soldier dies they are gone for good. This is but one example in XCOM 2 and teaches players the important lesson of
target priority and to figure which enemies are an immediate threat and which can be left for later.
